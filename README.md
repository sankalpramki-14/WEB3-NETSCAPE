# 🌐 NETSCAPE

## An Interactive Journey Through Web3

> **The internet evolved. So did the way we create, own, exchange, and connect.**

NETSCAPE isn't another page explaining Web3 with six paragraphs and a wall of definitions.

It is an **interactive Web3 experience** built around exploration, discovery, and interaction.

You don't just read about the internet.

**You enter it.**

---

# 🚀 THE JOURNEY

NETSCAPE takes you through the evolution of the internet:

**WEB1 → WEB2 → WEB3**

and turns that evolution into an interactive journey:

**WATCH → UNDERSTAND → EXPLORE → INTERACT → DISCOVER → CONNECT**

Along the way, you'll encounter the ideas behind the Web3 ecosystem through interactive visualizations and simulations.

# 🧩 SIX CONCEPTS. ONE NETWORK.

Explore the foundations of Web3 through interactive experiences:

### ⛓️ Blockchain
Discover how decentralized networks record and connect transactions.

### ⚡ Smart Contracts
See how programmable agreements can execute predefined rules.

### 💰 Cryptocurrency
Follow a digital transaction between **Alice and Bob** and understand how value can move across a network.

### 🖼️ NFTs
Explore the idea of unique digital assets, ownership, and verifiable identity.

### 🗳️ DAOs
Step into decentralized organizations and explore community-driven decision making.

### 👛 Wallets
Understand how wallets act as a gateway to interacting with blockchain-based ecosystems.
